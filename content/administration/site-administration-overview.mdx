### Site Administration Overview

Only an active Admin can open **System Settings > Site Administration**. The current hierarchy is:

- **General**, **Security** and **Uploads**;
- **Authentication**: General, OpenID Connect, Claim & Role Mapping, Account Links;
- **Email**: General and Email Templates;
- **Module Settings**: Backup Manager, Dashboard, DNS Manager, VLAN/IP Manager, Remote Manager, Secret Vault and Secure Send.

Admin-only navigation also includes **Team > Users**, **Data Management**, **Audit Logs** and **About Kaya**. Settings are primarily stored as database key/value rows, despite the historical internal table name `remote_manager_settings`.

Most saved database settings take effect without a restart. Environment settings, Compose ports and bind mounts require a container recreate. Test buttons can make network or email connections and are restricted in demo mode.
