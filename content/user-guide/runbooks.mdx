Runbooks capture the steps operators follow during common tasks, maintenance and incidents.

## Useful runbook pattern

1. State when the runbook should be used.
2. List prerequisites and access requirements.
3. Provide numbered steps with commands where needed.
4. Add rollback and verification steps.
5. Link related services and assets.
