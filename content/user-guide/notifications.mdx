Kaya keeps you informed about important events across your infrastructure, even when you are not actively using the application.

Depending on how your administrator has configured Kaya, notifications can be delivered through:

- In-app notifications
- PWA Push notifications
- Email notifications

## Notification Centre

Select the **bell icon** in the top-right corner of Kaya to open the notification centre.

Here you can:

- View unread notifications
- Mark notifications as read
- View notification history
- Open related resources directly

Notifications remain available in Kaya even if Push delivery fails.

## Notification severity

Notifications are grouped by severity.

| Severity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Info | General information |
| Success | Completed operations |
| Warning | Something needs attention |
| Critical | Failures or outages |

## Notification preferences

Open:

**Avatar → Notification Preferences**

Depending on administrator policy you can choose which notification channels are used for each event.

Available channels include:

- In-app
- Push
- Email

Some notifications may be mandatory and cannot be disabled.

## Enable Push notifications

To enable Push notifications:

1. Open Kaya on the device.
2. Open **Notification Preferences**.
3. Select **Enable Push Notifications**.
4. Allow browser notification permissions.
5. Enable Push for the events you wish to receive.

## iPhone & iPad

For Apple devices:

1. Open Kaya in Safari.
2. Select **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Kaya from the Home Screen icon.
4. Enable Push notifications.

Then confirm under:

**Settings → Notifications → Kaya**

that notifications are allowed.

## Android & Desktop

Most modern browsers support Push notifications directly.

Register each browser separately if you wish to receive notifications on multiple devices.

## Testing notifications

Your administrator may provide notification diagnostics.

Successful test notifications confirm:

- Push registration
- Device connectivity
- Notification permissions

They do not necessarily confirm that every Kaya module is publishing notification events correctly.

## Troubleshooting

If you don't receive notifications:

- Confirm Push is enabled
- Confirm notification permissions are granted
- Confirm the event is enabled
- Confirm your device is registered
- Confirm the notification appears inside Kaya

If a notification exists in Kaya but not on your device, contact your Kaya administrator.
