# Notifications

Kaya provides three notification channels:

-   In-app
-   PWA Push
-   Email

## Notification Centre

Use the bell icon beside your avatar to view unread notifications, mark
them as read and review notification history.

## Notification Preferences

Open **Avatar → Notification Preferences** to choose which events you
receive through each available channel.

## Enabling Push Notifications

1.  Open Kaya on the device.
2.  Open **Notification Preferences**.
3.  Enable Push Notifications.
4.  Accept the browser permission request.
5.  Enable Push for the required events.

## iPhone & iPad

Install Kaya using **Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen** before
enabling Push notifications.

## Troubleshooting

If notifications are not received:

-   Confirm the event was generated.
-   Confirm your device is registered.
-   Confirm notification permissions are enabled.
-   Confirm the notification appears in Kaya.

If the notification exists in Kaya but not on your device, contact your
administrator.
