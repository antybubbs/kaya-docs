---
title: "Applications"
description: "Track and document applications managed through Kaya."
order: 2
version: "current"
---

Use Applications to record the services your environment depends on.

Typical details include owner, purpose, URLs, deployment notes, dependencies and operational status.

<Callout type="tip" title="Good practice">
Link each application to related assets, runbooks and monitoring notes where possible.
</Callout>
